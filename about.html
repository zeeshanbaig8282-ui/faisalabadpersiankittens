Who We Are

Faisalabad Persian Kittens was founded by a family of dedicated cat enthusiasts who fell deeply in love with the Persian breed more than a decade ago. What began as a single pair of beautiful Persians has grown into a carefully managed, home-based cattery focused entirely on quality over quantity.

We are not a commercial factory — we are cat lovers first. Every litter is planned thoughtfully, every kitten is raised underfoot in our home, and every family who adopts from us becomes part of our extended community.

Our cats are our companions. They sleep in our beds, play in our living rooms, and thrive in a warm, stimulating environment. This is the only way we know how to breed — with love at the center of everything.

Why Our Kittens?

* Pure Persian breed with beautiful features.

* Raised in a clean, home environment (not cages).

* Healthy, active & Dewormed Kittens.

* Proper Litter & Well-trained Kittens.

* Fed with high-quality food for proper growth.

* Litter trained from an early age.

* Regular care, hygiene & grooming maintained.

* Friendly, playful & used to human interaction.

* No compromise on quality.

* Guidance provided for new pet owners.

* Serious care taken to ensure kittens go to loving homes.

Our Principles

What we stand for

01

Ethical Breeding

We breed only healthy adults with verified temperaments. We never overbred our queens, and each cat enjoys a full, enriched life.

02

Transparency

We welcome visits, share full health records, and answer every question honestly. You'll always know exactly what you're getting.

03

Lifetime Support

Adoption isn't the end — it's the beginning of a relationship. We're always available for advice, guidance, and support.

04

Carefully Matched

We take time to understand your lifestyle and match you with the kitten whose personality is the perfect fit for your home.
